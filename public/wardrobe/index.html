The $8,000 envelope

At $20,000 revenue, $12,000 contribution requires variable costs at or below $8,000. Est.

Garment manufacturing, materials, trims$5,900
Quality control, packaging, shipping$650
Fit services and wardrobe coordination$750
Customer acquisition and onboarding$700
Client contribution$12,000

This collection's manufacturing midpoint is $5,150 of the $5,900 materials cap. The remaining envelope is QC, fit, and acquisition — not more garment.
