How the Founding Edition works

Made after you choose it, never before.

  1. I

    Reserve

    Choose your colorway and size. Your pair is paid for today and given a founding number.

  2. II

    The run is confirmed

    When a colorway reaches 120 pairs, we place the order with our workshop in Felgueiras.

  3. III

    Made for you

    Each pair is cut, closed and lasted to order. We send photographs from the bench as it happens.

  4. IV

    Delivered

    Pairs ship June to July 2027, boxed with a spare set of laces and your numbered card.

Our promise: if a colorway does not reach 120 pairs, every reservation for it is refunded in full within five business days. No credit notes, no conditions.

The atelier

Felgueiras, northern Portugal.

Our pairs are developed and made in the Felgueiras footwear cluster, the heart of Portuguese shoemaking, where each shoe is cut, closed and lasted on site. Uppers are calf leather and woven textiles; every pair is lined in calf leather and finished by hand.

We make in runs of a few hundred pairs. That is small enough to number each one, and large enough that the price reflects the materials, not the scarcity.

Questions

What happens if a colorway does not reach its run?+

Every reservation for that colorway is refunded in full, to the original card, within five business days of March 2, 2027. Or you may move it to another colorway.

Why pre-order?+

Made-to-order means no overproduction, no end-of-season markdowns and no warehouse of unsold pairs. You get a pair made for you; we make only what is wanted.

How do they fit?+

True to size for most. If you are between sizes, take the half size up. The leather lining relaxes slightly with wear. Size exchanges are free within 30 days of delivery.

Can I cancel?+

Yes, for a full refund at any time before March 2, 2027, when the order goes to the workshop.

Will there be a sale?+

No. The price is the same for everyone, on the first day and the last.

Caring for suede and woven leather+

Brush with a soft crepe or nylon brush after wear. Treat once with a suede protector before the first wear. Store in the dust bag, away from direct sun.
