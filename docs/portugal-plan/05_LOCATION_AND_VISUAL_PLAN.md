## Creative direction
Use actual landscape and human movement: Atlantic light, white plaster, volcanic stone, blue tiles, walled gardens and quiet streets. Religious architecture supports the existing brand idea of grace and becoming. Show everyday clothing and footwear at a human scale. Avoid costume, an invented ecclesiastical endorsement or actors inserted into documentary worship footage.

Keep Saint red #B03236, midnight blue, ivory and charcoal. Add coastal blue and muted garden green through photography rather than proliferating UI colors. Serif for expressive headlines; plain readable body/form type. Preserve the wordmark and lowercase i. Product images must remain accurate and well lit even when editorial frames use stronger atmosphere. Do not add watery distortion to shoes or text.
