## Evidence standard
A manufacturer capability is not proof the brand has that service. A designer render is not an approved sample. A list price is not a confirmed factory cost. A fictional cast size is not a validated size run. A web page saying “made to order” is not an accepted supplier agreement.

## Existing charity copy
Observed exact sentence: “MADE SAiNT will dedicate 5% of annual net profits to supporting children in Cap-Haïtien, Haiti, through Brace for IMPACT 46.”
Keep the existing approved policy and basis; this pack neither verifies disbursements nor authorizes changing the percentage, recipient or net-profit denominator. If the founder is reviewing it separately, hold only the change, not invent a different pledge. Do not imply a named charity endorses a shoe or a Portuguese church endorses the brand.

## Legal/policy content
No new legal terms are supplied as publishable copy. Use actual approved privacy, shipping, refund/correction and order terms. A named policy link must work and accurately match current operation.
