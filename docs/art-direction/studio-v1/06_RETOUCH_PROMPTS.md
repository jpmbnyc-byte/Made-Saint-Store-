# Source-preserving retouch instructions

These are production briefs for a capable image editor/retoucher, not a claim that the current package contains processed replacements. The new preview-edit batch was rejected by the image service. Use the approved studio references and actual source masters; do not reinterpret the designs.

## Shared instruction

Edit the supplied source photograph. Change its photographic background, environment cast and lighting response to match the approved MADE SAiNT charcoal-studio reference. Preserve all actual subjects and products. Retain face/body identity, skin tone, hair, pose, hands, camera angle, silhouette, rise, pleats, hems, pockets, pattern placement, every crest and logo, shoe panels and wave-mark connections, sole/tread, laces, eyelets, cords and hardware. Preserve the original material and garment colors. Do not restyle or add new clothing, accessories, text, people or objects.

Create a quiet neutral charcoal seamless background with a subtle center-to-edge gradient and a matte charcoal floor. Use a broad soft key from upper camera-left with restrained fill. Keep shadows sculptural but open, ivory highlights textured, colors rich but accurate, and skin naturally detailed. Preserve genuine velvet nap, brushed wool, fine weave, mesh holes, satin reflections, intentional nylon crinkles and metallic finishes. Restore physically plausible contact shadows. Avoid halos, black voids, colored gels, warm scenery casts, fake bloom, excessive grain, oversharpening and plastic skin. Keep the existing composition and crop unless producing an explicitly specified responsive derivative.

## Family adjustments

| Family | Add to shared instruction |
| --- | --- |
| Full-length lookbook | Keep the complete existing outfit and natural stance. Match consistent apparent model scale across comparable cards. Keep face, hair, hems and footwear in the full-length master. Do not infer hidden clothing from other photos. |
| Cropped trouser product | Use the approved chest-to-shoe framing when the source supports it. Keep waistband, rise, pleat count, pockets, hem and footwear unobstructed. Do not crop a full-length campaign master automatically for every context. |
| Paired capsule | Preserve both original people and their left/right assignment, all garments and footwear. Build one studio scene and one shared key direction; avoid two separately pasted portraits. Derive all card/full/large variants from the same recovered master. |
| Campaign ensemble | Preserve every original person, expression, pose, outfit, prop and relative placement. Replace the scenic environment with one coherent studio stage. Retain seated support at the existing height with a simple matte neutral support. Maintain the existing wide crop and intentional negative space. |
| Private Wardrobe service | Preserve the human interaction with wardrobe boxes, folded clothing, garment bag and necessary counter/seat. Simplify background and surfaces into a quiet charcoal set; do not erase the delivery/fitting experience. |
| Shoe pair / profile / rear / overhead | Preserve exact shoe contour, panel boundaries, emblem, laces, eyelets, heel loop and hardware. Use slightly more fill to reveal construction and dark material. Every angle must depict the same actual colorway. Replace the ground/backdrop; do not change camera or create new unseen angles. |
| On-foot | Preserve feet, actual shoe scale, socks/trouser hem and perspective. Retain the selected model's anatomy. Replace only background/ground and lighting cast. Keep shadows under soles rather than floating shoes. |
| Macro / mesh / hardware | Protect exact weave/mesh density, stitching and finish. Do not invent texture or let a new gray background fill open mesh holes incorrectly. Reduce environmental tint locally; retain true metallic/reflection behavior. |
| Hanging apparel / bag | Keep original garment shape, hanger/hook, strap length, handle, zipper and hardware. Remove busy retail context through accurate masking. Maintain realistic shadows and material. Do not use generic ghost-mannequin anatomy or redesign the product. |
| Front/back / multi-view plate | Obtain separate clean cells. Retouch photographic backgrounds consistently, then rebuild the exact plate order and labels. Keep front and back colors matched. Do not darken all text/technical graphics as part of the photo grade. |
| Process collage | Preserve shoe sketches, technical linework, sample tags and true material swatches. Retouch photographs cell by cell; retain readable graphic fields. If originals are missing, recover them before rebuilding. |
| Headwrap / M charm | Keep the true opening, concealed housing, cord length/alignment, lock, hair and mesh-over-satin layering. Neutralize the background, not the material's actual multicolor light response. No invented mechanism. |

## Negative constraints to append

No redesign, no variant swap, no new branding, no changed crest, no extra eyelets, no new sole, no disconnected shoe emblem, no invisible pockets or changed pleats, no face replacement, no hair change, no skin whitening, no smoothing away texture, no fake embroidery, no repeated generic fabric pattern, no copied reference outfit, no new location claim, no screenshot UI, no watermark.

## Output record for each master

Record the exact original and master-source path/hash, edit tool/version or retoucher, mask/source provenance, output dimensions, role profile, derivative paths, all usage assignments, date, and the six QA checks in the manifest. A rejection or missing source is a recorded blocker, not permission to substitute a generic generated asset.
