Sourcing verdicts

  • 4On card. ΔE under 2.5: order the card color, confirm on the physical swatch.
  • 24Lab dip. ΔE 2.5 to 8: same article, request a lab dip to the target.
  • 0Dye to target. ΔE over 8: nothing close on the card, custom dye with a minimum.
  • 10Trim spec. Not in the MEES library. Specify the target to MEES for trim sourcing.

Read before sending to MEES

  • Card colors are measured from MEES photos on Google Drive. Lighting shifts them, so every ΔE is a starting point. Confirm against physical swatch cards.
  • Texture families are a visual read of each card. Confirm the article, weight, and minimum with MEES before costing.
  • Sole compound colors are pigment-matched by the sole maker. The verdict there tells you how far the target sits from their standard colors.
  • The tongue carries a stacked MADE / SAiNT printed straight onto the leather, repeated on the sockliner. There is no woven label or emblem.
  • Wordmark is MADE SAiNT with a lowercase i. The runner concept image shows an uppercase I. The flats follow the brand rule.
  • Duty on footwear into the US depends on the HTS classification of the upper (textile, leather, or synthetic). The 20% default is a placeholder.
  • No style codes or SKUs are assigned here. Look 03 reuses the runner flat until a third locked flat is supplied.
