Marketing plan

Positioning. MADE SAiNT is the quiet runner for people who know Cucinelli and buy Bogner: calf-lined, made to order in Portugal, numbered, and never discounted. It costs half of what the Italian houses ask, because we make only what is reserved.

Quiet, not loud

No logo on the upper beyond the tongue wordmark. Cucinelli's restraint, priced where a Bogner customer can say yes.

Made after you choose it

Made-to-order is the luxury, not a funding mechanism. Say it plainly: we make only what is reserved.

Numbered, never discounted

Each founding pair carries its number. One price, first day to last. No codes, no sale, no outlet.

Proof of the bench

Show the hands and the materials. Every claim (calf lining, hand finishing) is photographed in the workshop.
